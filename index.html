The Sacred Journey of Light Across 7 Acts

✨ Spiritual Philosophy of Deepavali

Diwali (Deepavali, from Dīpa = lamp, Āvalī = array) is far more than a festival of lights; it is an inward pilgrimage. Across seven sacred days in the month of Ashvin and Kartika (Vikram Samvat), each dawn and dusk awakens a distinct facet of human consciousness—from ecological gratitude (Vasu Baras) and holistic health (Dhanteras), to the vanquishing of ego (Naraka Chaturdashi), spiritual radiance (Lakshmi Pujan), renewed life (Padwa), and unconditional sibling love (Bhau Beej).

🌌 Pure 3D WebGL Particle Animation (ORIGIN Architecture)

Inspired by ORIGIN, ANANTADEEPA renders each sacred act as a pure 30,000 living particle constellation with zero 2D flat image planes. Every single figure—Shri Rama and Sita, Gau Mata and Calf, the Three Golden Kalash, Shri Krishna defeating Narakasura, Goddess Mahalakshmi on Lotus, Lord Vamana and King Bali, and the Sister's Aarti Blessing—is constructed directly from the high-resolution master artwork coordinates, floating in 3D stereoscopic depth with thermal buoyancy, breathing flickers, pointer repulsion, and sparkler shockwaves.

🎶 Procedural Web Audio Synthesis

Zero external audio files are loaded. The Tanpura drone (tuned to meditative D), the resonant physical modal partials of the Temple Bell (Ghanta), and the crackling bursts of Diwali Phuljhadi (sparklers) are 100% synthesized procedurally using the Web Audio API.
